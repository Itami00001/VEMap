# CShapes 2.0 historical extracts

The files named `cshapes-*.geojson` are extracts of the Gleditsch–Ward
entity 365 in CShapes 2.0. Each file keeps the original geometry and validity
interval, with coordinate precision reduced to five decimal places.
The website selects the snapshot at the middle of the chosen month or at
the end of an annual period. The extracts depict the dated dataset's
political boundaries and do not model military front lines.

Source: [ETH Zürich International Conflict Research, CShapes](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/cshapes/).

Schvitz, Guy, Seraina Rüegger, Luc Girardin, Lars-Erik Cederman, Nils
Weidmann, and Kristian Skrede Gleditsch. 2022. “Mapping the International
System, 1886–2019: The CShapes 2.0 Dataset.” *Journal of Conflict
Resolution* 66 (1), 144–161. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00220027211013563).
